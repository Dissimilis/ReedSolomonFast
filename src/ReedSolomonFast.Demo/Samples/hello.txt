Hello from the ReedSolomonFast demo.

This file is split into four data shards, two parity shards are computed, two of the
six are deleted, and the file is rebuilt from the remaining four. Any four of the six
would do, whichever ones survive.

A few non-ASCII characters, so some letters take more than one byte:
Ačiū, señor, Grüße, naïve café, 日本語, Ελληνικά.
